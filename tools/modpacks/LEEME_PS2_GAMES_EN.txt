PUT YOUR COPIES OF THE PS2 / PSP GAMES HERE (to import characters)
==================================================================

Both USA and Europe (PAL) versions work. The launcher's importer finds them by
file name; it only needs to contain:

- Budokai 1:  "Budokai (" in an .iso
              example: DragonBall Z - Budokai (Europe) (En,Fr,De,Es,It).iso
- Budokai 2:  "Budokai 2" in an .iso
- Infinite World:  "Infinite World" in an .iso, or in a FOLDER with the disc
              extracted inside (with USR\DATA_CMN.AFS)
- Shin Budokai (PSP): "Shin Budokai" in an .iso
              example: Dragonball Z Shin Budokai (USA).iso
- Shin Budokai Another Road (PSP): "Another Road" in an .iso
- Budokai 3 (PS2): "Budokai 3" in an .iso, or in a folder with the disc extracted.
              Used for the base moves of Budokai 1 characters and for Shin Budokai
              moves; without it the base character's moves are used.

ISOs are read as they are: no need to extract them.
Tested with: Budokai 1 and 2 (Europe), Infinite World, Shin Budokai and Another
Road (USA). Other regions are found the same way (by content) but are not tested
yet: if one does not work, tell us on Discord.
