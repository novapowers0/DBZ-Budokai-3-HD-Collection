## English

| Download | What it is |
|---|---|
| `DBZ-Budokai-3-HD-Collection-v1.5.5.zip` | **Windows game** (required). |
| `...-v1.5.5-linux-amd64.tar.gz` | Linux game (Vulkan, beta). |

**Install / update:** unzip over your installation **overwriting everything**, open `dbz3.exe` and press **PLAY**. Settings, mods and saves are kept.

**What's new**
- 🚧 **Infinite World (experimental)**: all 96 missions are playable in the US and European versions.
- 🚧 **Budokai 2 (experimental)**:
  - the board starts without crashes and asks "Is this okay?" after choosing an ally, like the original;
  - Babidi's ship: all four floors work and you can return after editing skills;
  - the Red Ribbon base no longer crashes the game.
- 🥊 **Legend of Mr. Satan**: round 3 introduction and per-rival music.
- 🧑 **Hero Mode (beta)**:
  - sagas 1-3 playable (4-8 in development); the Androids and Piccolo sagas now take place on Earth;
  - choose **Budokai 3** or **Xenoverse 2** parts in the creator (experimental, only if XV2 is installed);
  - Budokai 3 hairstyles (beta).
- 🌐 **Online (beta)**: fights stay in sync in testing; automatic reconnect when mods don't match.
- 🔧 **Performance and fixes**: 480p (640x480) internal resolution option for modest PCs; complete translations.

**Known:** online has not been tested yet in long matches between two homes. Linux not fully tested. Some added characters still show no aura.
