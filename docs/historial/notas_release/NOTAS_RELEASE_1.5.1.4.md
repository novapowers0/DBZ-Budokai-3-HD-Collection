## English

| Download | What it is |
|---|---|
| `DBZ-Budokai-3-HD-Collection-v1.5.1.4.zip` | **Windows game** (required). |
| `...-v1.5.1.4-linux-amd64.tar.gz` | Linux game (Vulkan). |
| `DBZ3HD-1.5.1.4-Kit-Modding.zip` | Optional: modding tools, Python included. |

**Install / update:** unzip over your installation **overwriting everything**, open `dbz3.exe` and press **PLAY**. Settings, mods and saves are kept.

**What's new**
- 🚧 **Budokai 2 (experimental)**:
  - the board uses the same framing as on PS2;
  - Gotenks, Vegito and Shenron on the board, and pieces with their animations;
  - dialogue uses the original font and portraits;
  - Babidi's ship has the original sky and clouds.
- 🚧 **Infinite World (experimental)**:
  - flight minigames work with the European ISO;
  - the red bar minigame has its original intro camera and background;
  - the map is correctly placed in 4:3, 16:10 and 21:9.
- 🧑 **Hero Mode (beta)**:
  - character list with duplicate and archive;
  - 3D character preview with several cameras;
  - new save format: your characters are converted automatically;
  - change movesets in game, without restarting.
- 🥊 **Legend of Mr. Satan**: round 1 with the original timing and subtitles.
- 🔧 **Performance and fixes**:
  - the HD pack no longer crashes the game when toggled on and off;
  - new **performance mode** for modest GPUs;
  - the launcher warns when PS2 stages are not found.

**Known:** some added characters still show no aura. On 2-core PCs the game doesn't reach 60 FPS.
