## English

| Download | What it is |
|---|---|
| `DBZ-Budokai-3-HD-Collection-v1.5.5.1.zip` | **Windows game** (required). |
| `...-v1.5.5.1-linux-amd64.tar.gz` | Linux game (Vulkan, beta). |

**Install / update:** unzip over your installation **overwriting everything**. Settings, mods and saves are kept.

**What's new**
- 🌐 **Online (beta)**, easier to follow:
  - 4 visible steps: host or join, share the code, connecting, fight;
  - connection status with colour and ping;
  - **X** copies or pastes the room code;
  - errors tell you what to do, and B always takes you back;
  - if a desync happens, the game saves a report in the `informes_online` folder: **please send it to us**.
- 🧑 **Hero Mode**: 1.5.5 did not install the saga data (Earth stages and parts catalogue). Fixed.

**Help us test the online!** Play with a friend and tell us how it goes in an issue.
