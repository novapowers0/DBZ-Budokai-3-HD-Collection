## English

| Download | What it is |
|---|---|
| `DBZ-Budokai-3-HD-Collection-v1.5.1.zip` | **Windows game** (required). |
| `...-v1.5.1-linux-amd64.tar.gz` | Linux game (Vulkan). |
| `DBZ3HD-1.5.1-Kit-Modding.zip` | Optional: modding tools, Python included. |

**Install / update:** unzip over your install **overwriting everything**, open `dbz3.exe` and press **PLAY**. Settings, mods and saves are kept.

**What's new**
- 🚧 **Budokai 2 campaign (EXPERIMENTAL)**: red arrow, life column, portraits, sounds, music and Budokai 2 stages on every tile; Saibamen sprout from the ground. **Babidi's Ship (beta)** closer to the original.
- 🚧 **Infinite World campaign (EXPERIMENTAL)**: map borders, music per area and smoother animations.
- 🆕 **Hero mode (beta)**: change your hero's moveset inside the game.
- 🌍 **New languages**: Portuguese (Brazil) and Japanese.
- 🧩 **Nova's Mods**: Janemba and the Ginyu Force with their own voices; character slots are kept between versions.
- 🏟️ **Stage select**: the IW minigame fields are no longer listed, so every new stage fits (Snowy Mountains was missing).
- ⚡ **Faster**: building the mod characters takes less than half the time and portraits are no longer redrawn on every update.
- 🔧 Fixed: on some PCs Nova's Mods and the update check did not load.

**Known:** some added characters (Janemba, Guldo…) still show no aura; Future Gohan's transformation auras are still pending.
