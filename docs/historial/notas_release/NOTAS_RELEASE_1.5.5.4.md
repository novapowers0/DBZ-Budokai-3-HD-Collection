- 🎬 **Leyenda de Mr. Satán**: las presentaciones de cada ronda usan ahora las cámaras de Budokai 1: los luchadores salen enteros, sin bandas negras y sin recortes.

- 🛡️ **Más estable**: si el juego se cerraba a mitad de preparar los personajes, ya no se queda bloqueado; un mod con una cápsula mal escrita ya no cierra el juego; los guardados de ajustes, perfiles y progreso ya no se estropean si se va la luz.
- ⚠️ **Aviso**: la primera vez que abras el juego tras actualizar, los escenarios de PS2 se preparan de nuevo. Tarda un poco; es normal.

## English

| Download | What it is |
|---|---|
| `DBZ-Budokai-3-HD-Collection-v1.5.5.4.zip` | **Windows game** (required). |
| `...-v1.5.5.4-linux-amd64.tar.gz` | Linux game (Vulkan, beta). |

**Install / update:** unzip over your installation **overwriting everything**. Settings, mods and saves are kept.

**What's new**
- 🎬 **Mr. Satan's legend**: each round's introduction now uses Budokai 1's cameras: fighters appear whole, with no black bars and no cut-off edges.
- 🥋 **Mr. Satan's legend**: poses are closer to the original. Trunks wears his armour, Ten Shin Han has no shirt and Vegeta wears his armour, as in Budokai 1. No Next or Skip prompt during the shots.
- 🐉 **Infinite World (Dragon Mission)**: minigame cameras are back to the original: buttons, Kienzan shot, energy beams, Ten's shot against Cell, the field and the flight.
- 🤝 **Tag Team (beta)**: the partner controlled by the computer now swaps in when its fighter is low on health.
- 🎵 **Dragon World (Budokai 2) and Babidi's ship**: fixed the game's background music playing underneath the Budokai 2 music.
- 🌐 **Online (beta)**: rematches now start in sync as a new battle for both players.
- 🛡️ **More stable**: if the game closed while preparing the characters it no longer stays stuck; a mod with a badly written capsule no longer crashes the game; settings, profiles and progress saves no longer get corrupted on a power cut.
- ⚠️ **Notice**: the first time you open the game after updating, the PS2 stages are prepared again. It takes a little while; this is normal.
