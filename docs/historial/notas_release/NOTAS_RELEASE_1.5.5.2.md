**Install / update:** unzip over your installation **overwriting everything**. Settings, mods and saves are kept.

**What's new**
- 🧑 **Hero Mode (beta)**: saga 4 is playable; sagas 2, 3 and 4 now take place on the Earth map (it was always Namek before); Budokai 3 hairstyles that fit the head: Goku, Vegeta, Gohan (Cell) and Kid Goku (Bardock removed: the face showed through the back); fixed changing the moveset in the creator not applying the new moves in battle.
- 🥊 **Legend of Mr. Satan**: fixed Mr. Satan and the ring floor being invisible in the final Cell ring scene (bug in 1.5.5 and 1.5.5.1); Mr. Satan now squints in that scene like in the original.
- 🌐 **Online (beta)**: the desync report is now saved in `informes_online`; clear message if the host doesn't answer; shorter texts when mods don't match; if the connection drops mid-fight you can pause and quit.

**Help us test the online!** If you see a desync, send us the report from `informes_online` in an issue.
